Method
The lower limit is the lower of the average resolution rate to date (10 resolved over 3 working days = 3.3) and the average over the last 3 working days (the same 3 days, so also 3.3). Because 22 Oct counts as 0, the estimate leans low rather than high.
WARNINGIntake is about 1.5 times the lower limit. If both rates hold, the open work will grow by about 1.7 cards per working day (estimate).
